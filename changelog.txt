Version: 2.1.0
Date: 24. 08. 2026
  Changes:
    - Redrew the item and technology icons at the sizes the game actually wants: the item icon
      is now 64x64 instead of 32x32, and the technology icon 256x256 instead of 128x128, both
      with the mipmaps every base-game icon ships. Neither is upscaled in game any more.
    - The new icons are Factorio's own cliff-explosives art with the red and blue channels
      swapped, so the dynamite is yellow rather than blue. See CREDITS.md for provenance;
      thanks to the mod portal contributor who suggested the approach.
    - Retitled to "Explosive Excavation Continued". This is now a maintained continuation of
      GotLag's mod rather than a stopgap compatibility patch, so the title no longer says
      "unofficial" or names a specific Factorio version. The internal mod name is unchanged,
      so this is a normal update and existing saves are unaffected.
    - Added LICENSE (MIT, continuing GotLag's) and CREDITS.md, which records that the icons
      are derived from Wube's cliff-explosives art and are not covered by the MIT licence.
    - Replaced the thumbnail. The old one had "Unofficial for 2.1" painted across it, which
      no longer describes the mod. The new one is a four-way mosaic of the fills the charge
      actually produces - lava on Vulcanus, brash ice on Aquilo, heavy oil on Fulgora and a
      lake on Gleba - with the charge itself in the middle.

Version: 2.0.0
Date: 09. 08. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed that the mod failed to load on Factorio 2.1.7 and later: RecipePrototype `category` was removed and merged into `categories`.
    - Fixed that the item and technology icons pointed at `__Explosive Excavation__`, which is not this mod's name, so sprite loading failed.
    - Fixed that blasting charges could be placed on space platform foundation; the tiles are now restored and the charges refunded.
  Changes:
    - Declared dependencies: base >= 2.1.14, optional Space Age, and incompatible with the original Explosive Excavation mod.
    - Removed an unused icon and shrank the thumbnail from 1024x1024 to 256x256, cutting the download from 1.4 MB to about 180 KB.
    - Planets are now identified through the surface's planet prototype instead of the surface name, so renamed and scripted surfaces behave correctly.
    - The item now sorts after the Space Age terrain items instead of tying with artificial yumako soil.
